### decision-001
Date: 2026-07-30
Phase: Phase 3 — full outline (user gate)
What changed: contradiction-001 (deep-work block length) → framing C — **recommend 90–120 min block but explicitly flag the uncertainty** in the chapter body.
Why: Evidence is mixed. Ericsson describes 60–90 min sessions among elite *deliberate-practice* performers (not knowledge workers at desks); Csikszentmihalyi gives no specific block length; the 90–120 min ultradian cycle originates from sleep-stage research (Hobson & Pace-Schott 2002), not waking attention. Framing C matches the intake's "evidence-honest" posture — uses the conventional number but tells the reader the direct evidence is thinner than it looks. Recommended by master; user confirmed.

### decision-002
Date: 2026-07-30
Phase: Phase 3 — full outline (user gate)
What changed: contradiction-002 (morning routine duration) → framing A — **structured start, duration loose**. The 30-min-or-less ceiling is offered as a convention the reader can adjust, not as an evidence-backed optimum. Affects ch-01 (primary) and ch-03 (justification-only — the four-step ritual itself is unaffected).
Why: The "specific duration = productivity" claim for morning routines rests on the ego-depletion model (Baumeister), which failed pre-registered multi-lab replication (Hagger 2016, N≈2,000). Only the *structured start* is supported by evidence. Framing A softens the duration claim entirely while keeping the four-step ritual intact. Recommended by master; user confirmed.
