### 1. [HIGH] Locked decision "Idempotent indexer" violated for 2 of 8 tables — `frozen_line_occurrences` and `continuity_anchors` accumulate duplicates on every re-index

**Evidence:**
- `book-kit/mcp/book-kg/indexer.py:100-103` (continuity_anchors INSERT loop): no DELETE before INSERT, no UNIQUE constraint on the table
- `book-kit/mcp/book-kg/indexer.py:163-177` (frozen-lines loop): no DELETE for `frozen_line_occurrences` before INSERT, no UNIQUE constraint on the table
- `book-kit/mcp/book-kg/schema.sql:45-52` (frozen_line_occurrences CREATE TABLE): no UNIQUE constraint
- `book-kit/mcp/book-kg/schema.sql:88-97` (continuity_anchors CREATE TABLE): no UNIQUE constraint
- My plant test: `before reindex {'chapters': 3, 'beats': 3, 'motif_mentions': 6, 'character_mentions': 3, 'frozen_lines': 1, 'frozen_line_occurrences': 1, 'chapter_refs': 1, 'continuity_anchors': 1}` → `after reindex: {frozen_line_occurrences: 2, continuity_anchors: 2}` (delta +1 each). The existing coder test `test_idempotent_reindex_has_no_duplicates` at `book-kit/tests/test_book_kg.py:58-65` only asserts 4 of 8 tables.

**Impact:**
- Spec says: "Migration strategy: versioned schema (`schema_version` table) so re-indexes never crash on old DBs. Indexer is idempotent: re-running drops nothing, only inserts missing rows + updates hashes." (`share/notes/02_plan_T-2026-08-05-001_book-kit-roadmap.md:538`).
- Locked decision: "Idempotent indexer" (dispatch header).
- The orchestrator runs the indexer after Phase 2 and after each `am-coder` writing dispatch (`agents_manager/book-gen-orchestrator/SKILL.md:222-225`). For a 5-chapter book: 1 + 5 = 6 re-runs. After 6 runs: 1 occurrence → 6 rows in `frozen_line_occurrences`; 1 anchor → 6 rows in `continuity_anchors`.
- `query.py:contradicts()` at line 84-94 LEFT JOINs `continuity_anchors ca ON ca.book_id=fl.book_id` — a CROSS JOIN per frozen-line occurrence. After 6 runs with 1 occurrence and 1 anchor: 6 identical rows in the output, where the spec contract is 1 row. The MCP tool's `contradicts()` output is inflated by the bug.

**Why this is HIGH not MEDIUM:**
- Violates a LOCKED decision (the dispatch's bullet list calls it out by name).
- Affects a primary MCP tool's output (`contradicts()`) — not a benign internal artifact.
- Manifests on every re-index, not just on a corner case.
- Pre-existing 7/7 test suite does NOT catch it (test gap; coder test only asserts 4 of 8 tables).

**Suggested fix (in the fix loop):** pick ONE of the two approaches per table — they're equivalent in cost:
- Option A (DELETE-then-INSERT, matches the indexer's existing pattern for beats/motif_mentions/character_mentions):
  - `indexer.py`: add a per-book `DELETE FROM frozen_line_occurrences WHERE chapter_id IN (SELECT id FROM chapters WHERE book_id=?)` (or `WHERE frozen_line_id IN (SELECT id FROM frozen_lines WHERE book_id=?)`) inside the frozen-lines loop, BEFORE the insert.
  - `indexer.py`: add a per-book `DELETE FROM continuity_anchors WHERE book_id=?` inside the bible section, BEFORE the insert.
- Option B (UNIQUE constraint, matches `frozen_lines` and `motifs` pattern):
  - `schema.sql`: add `UNIQUE(frozen_line_id, chapter_id, line_number)` to `frozen_line_occurrences` and `UNIQUE(book_id, keyword, scope_start_chapter, scope_end_chapter)` to `continuity_anchors`. The existing `INSERT OR IGNORE` will then dedupe.

Either approach is 2-3 lines. The existing test should be extended to assert delta=0 for both tables (test_book_kg.py:58-65 already runs the re-index; just add 2 more asserts).

### 7. [LOW] `ch-NN` regex `^ch-(\d{1,4})$` accepts `ch-0` and `ch-1` (no zero-padding) — over-permissive but harmless

**Evidence:**
- `book-kit/mcp/book-kg/query.py:17`: `CHAPTER_ID = re.compile(r"^ch-(\d{1,4})$", re.I)`

**Impact:** `ch-0` and `ch-1` and `ch-001` and `ch-1` all parse to `chapter_num=1`. The query then matches against `c.chapter_num` so `ch-0` would never find a real chapter (chapter_num starts at 1) and returns empty. `ch-001` parses to 1, finds ch-01, returns its data. Not a security issue; not a correctness bug; just a slight UX gap. The output always normalizes to `'ch-' || printf('%02d', c.chapter_num)`, so callers see `ch-01` regardless of input.

**Suggested fix:** tighten to `^ch-0?([1-9]\d?|0)$` (require 1-99, zero-padded) OR leave as-is and document. Cosmetic; auto-acceptable.
