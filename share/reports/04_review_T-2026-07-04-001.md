## Smart-deviation call — WARN-8 frame-0 anchor

**am-coder's deviation:** The AG16 audit at `share/reports/04_review_T-2026-07-04-001.md` (am-design's section, WARN-8) recommended adding `tabindex="-1"` to **4** anchor locations: `:651, :658, :666, :667` (one anchor per active CTA, one per hidden CTAs in frames 1 and 2). am-coder patched **3** locations (`:658, :666, :667`), deliberately skipping `:651` (frame-0 active CTA).

**Ratify or override?**

**Ratify.** I read the JS at `templates/cinematic-landing/skeleton/index.html:1058-1067`:

```js
function show(i){
  idx = ((i % frames.length) + frames.length) % frames.length;
  frames.forEach((f, n) => {
    const on = n === idx;
    f.classList.toggle("is-shown", on);
    f.classList.toggle("is-hidden-up", !on);
    f.setAttribute("aria-hidden", on ? "false" : "true");
  });
  …
}
```

Every `show()` call sets `aria-hidden="false"` on the active frame and `"true"` on the others. Frame-0's anchor at `:651` is in the *active* frame on page load — `aria-hidden` is `"false"` on initial paint and remains `"false"` until the user clicks a dot. axe-core flagged 2 nodes (the aria-hidden frames 1 + 2), not 3 — frame-0's anchor was correctly NOT in the flagged set.

Adding `tabindex="-1"` to frame-0's anchor would:
1. Make Tab skip the primary CTA on initial page load (the entire point of having a primary CTA as the page's focal point).
2. Miss the axe-flagged issue entirely (axe didn't flag it).

am-design's "4 anchors" was an over-citation of the fix scope; the substantive issue is *non-active* CTA anchors in aria-hidden subtrees. am-coder patched exactly those.

**One caveat for future-follow-up, not material:** When frames 1 or 2 are active on a later user interaction, frame-0's anchor temporarily carries `aria-hidden="true"` and is still Tab-reachable. A fully clean fix would dynamic-toggle `tabindex` alongside the `is-shown` toggle. This is "nice-to-have," not "must-fix," and the deviation is the smaller safer change.

**Net: ratify the deviation. Frame-0 stays Tab-reachable.**
