A future caller wanting to write the artifact next to the chapter (e.g. `books/daily-focus/.gate-ch-01.md`) works; one trying to write to `share/reports/...` is rejected. The default path (no `--out`) bypasses the check and goes to `share/reports/<task>/02_gate_*.md` directly. If the orchestrator paragraph at SKILL.md:137-152 needs to allow the orchestrator to override, the guard can be relaxed with a `--no-path-check` flag, but that's a future change.
